        Road to the platinum: Vice Got a Price!

        About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10, one playthrough, no mission replay and no New Game+. The order below is built from the official descriptions and the cited guides: story first, then the side strands, then collectibles, then the long activity grind. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter (15 slots) and one from just before the final mission. Never use cheats on your trophy save — they disable trophies and saving.

        ⚠ One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude. Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1, before the chapter ends. Every other trophy stays earnable after the story.
